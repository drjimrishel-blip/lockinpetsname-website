Nutrition, exercise & health

Food: Choose a complete and balanced food appropriate for the dog's age, size, and health. Ask a veterinarian about breed-specific needs and special diets.

Exercise: Tailor daily activity to age, health, and the individual dog's energy. Combine physical exercise with play and mental enrichment.

Grooming: Coat type, skin condition, and lifestyle affect brushing and grooming frequency. Check ears, teeth, nails, and skin regularly.

Veterinary care: Ask about preventive care, screening, vaccinations, and common inherited conditions for this particular breed.
